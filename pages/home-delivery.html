Simple process

How to order, step by step

  1. 1. Choose your items

    Browse our products and add them to your list, or just note down the medicine names.

  2. 2. Send your enquiry

    Fill in the short enquiry form. Upload a photo of your prescription if you have one.

  3. 3. We call to confirm

    Our pharmacist checks your order, confirms the price and agrees a delivery time with you.

  4. 4. Pay your way

    Pay safely online before delivery, or choose Cash on Delivery (COD) when your medicines arrive.

  5. 5. Receive at home

    Check your pack, keep the bill, and store the medicines as written on the label.

Ask us

You can pay by card or UPI online, or pay Cash on Delivery when your medicines arrive. We will confirm the total amount before we send your order.

Most orders inside our delivery area arrive the same day. Orders confirmed after 6:00 pm may arrive the next morning. Delivery times can change on public holidays or in bad weather.

For safety, we cannot take back medicines once they have left our store, unless we sent the wrong item, the pack is damaged or the medicine is past its expiry date. Please call us within 24 hours and we will make it right.

Every order is sealed in a clean, tamper-evident bag. Medicines that need to stay cool are packed with a cold pack in an insulated bag. Please store them as written on the label as soon as they arrive.

Prescription medicines are given only against a valid prescription from a registered doctor. Please send a clear photo or PDF of the prescription. Our pharmacist may call you to check the details, and our delivery person may ask to see the original.
