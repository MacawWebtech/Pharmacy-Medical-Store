# Support

## Common questions

**The form says "sent" but I get no email.** The form is in demo mode until you set a real endpoint. See `customization.md`, section Forms.

**Fonts or icons do not appear.** They load from the internet. Check your connection, or switch to local copies (see `installation.md`).

**Dark mode or RTL did not change.** Clear the saved choice: in your browser console run `localStorage.clear()`, or clear site data in browser settings.

**The map is a grey box.** Add your Google Maps embed link in `Config.mapEmbedUrl` in `assets/js/main.js`.
